Overview

One app for the whole construction workflow

BuildTech helps users manage construction work from idea to execution. Clients request plans and construction work, follow their projects and estimate costs. Suppliers list products and manage orders. Admins manage the platform.

The challenge

Many parties, scattered tools

Construction projects involve clients, engineers and suppliers, and each step from plans to costs to materials is often handled separately.

Our solution

Clients, engineers and suppliers in one place

We designed and built an Arabic-first mobile app with separate experiences for clients and suppliers, multi-step request forms, project tracking, a marketplace and consultations.

Key features

What the app does

  • 01

    Plan requests

    A multi-step form for architectural, structural, electrical, plumbing, interior and full-package plans.

  • 02

    Construction requests

    Project details, location and budget-related fields in a single flow.

  • 03

    Ownership documents

    Upload proof of ownership to private cloud storage.

  • 04

    Project tracking

    Follow each project and its status updates.

  • 05

    Marketplace and orders

    Browse products and use a cart, while suppliers manage their products and orders.

  • 06

    Consultations and cost calculator

    Book a consultation and estimate costs inside the app.
