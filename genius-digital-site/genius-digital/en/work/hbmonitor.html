HBMonitor

Mobile health

A mobile health app that estimates hemoglobin levels from a guided camera scan, for screening and monitoring.

Sector
Mobile health (mHealth)
Platform
Mobile app, React Native
Backend
Supabase
Interface
Arabic and English

Overview

Hemoglobin screening with a phone camera

HBMonitor guides the user to capture an image, checks its quality, and estimates a hemoglobin level on the device using color analysis and a machine-learning model. Results are classified as normal, mild, moderate or severe, adjusted for sex, age and pregnancy status, and saved so users can follow trends.

The challenge

Checking hemoglobin usually needs a blood test

Hemoglobin is normally measured with a blood sample taken at a lab or clinic. HBMonitor explores a non-invasive way to screen and monitor it from a phone.

For screening and research purposes only. Not a replacement for laboratory tests or medical advice.

Our solution

A guided scan that works on the device

We built a mobile app with a step-by-step camera guide, on-device image quality checks and analysis, and a clear result screen with history and trends, in Arabic and English.

Key features

What the app does

  • 01

    Guided camera scan

    Step-by-step alignment with on-device checks for brightness, focus and redness.

  • 02

    On-device analysis

    Color features such as RGB ratios and a redness index are analyzed locally on the phone.

  • 03

    Personalized classification

    Severity (normal, mild, moderate, severe) adjusted for sex, age and pregnancy status.

  • 04

    Trends and history

    Weekly averages, highest and lowest readings, and a chart of past results.

  • 05

    Accounts and sync

    Secure sign-in and profile synchronization with Supabase.

Screens

Scan, result and trends
